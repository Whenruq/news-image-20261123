One image summing up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026, built
for make.fun's "Sum up 23 November's world news in one image" bounty.

The front page leads with tankers ablaze in the Strait of Hormuz after
striking naval mines, with panels for Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as
CEO as its shares soared about 30%, the Dymer toll reaching 11 with a day
of mourning in the Kyiv region, Kyiv's claim of sinking a Russian warship
in Libya, the COP31 collapse in Antalya, and the search resuming at the
Cikande candy factory. The ticker adds Brent crude near a three-month low
and the US probe into suspected cyberattacks on energy tankers.

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is a
1024x1024 JPEG (by magic bytes) under 1 MiB, written byte-identically
on every run.
